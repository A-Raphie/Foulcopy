## Product
Speak once. See what you said and what you meant to send.

- Axis pushes:
  - Color: monochrome ink + one sponsor accent (Get started violet `#3923c7`, mined Sep 14 2026).

  - Layout: document / proof sheet. The product IS two columns of type.
- Axes held conventional: Geist + Geist Mono, one header row, landing then /app split.

- Signature move: THE PROOF SHEET. Spoken vs send ready with strike/add marks bound to `text` and `llm_response`. Mechanism test: it is the Dictation API pair. Five-minute test: the DIFF geometry plus live PCM is not a shadcn split. Demo: this is the money frame.
- Ledger check: last entries Claimcheck (warm paper evidence trail) and Curb (mono-first live tape). This is light proof-sheet, not a tape and not a verdict trail.
- Clone test: cream + violet is theirs; the DIFF columns and dropped/added counts are not on assemblyai.com (their /transcribe URL is a 405).

## Consensus default (banned)
Dark voice-note app, purple orb mic, waveform, chat bubble transcript, "powered by AssemblyAI".

## Avoid-list
Purple-to-blue gradients. Glass orbs. Waveform decoration. Chat bubbles. Dark neon. Bento. Emoji feature icons. Mono headlines. Em dashes in copy.

## Familiarity anchor

- App: opener line + controls + full-width proof sheet.
